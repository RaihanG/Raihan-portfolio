Research

What I work on

Robust, interpretable AI that survives real clinical noise — bridging optics, signal processing, and deep learning.

AI in Optics PhD focus

Physics-informed AI for optical tissue characterization under noisy acquisition — building a translational SFDI-based optical-mapping pipeline for endoscopic imaging.

  • Robustness to device variability
  • Clinically usable optical-property estimation
  • Model interpretability & evaluation

Medical Image Segmentation

Attention-driven deep architectures (ECA-SKFNet) for cerebrovascular vessel segmentation from TOF-MRA, plus brain-tumour and lung segmentation work.

  • Robust feature extraction
  • Attention & transfer learning
  • Clinically relevant analysis

Biomedical Signal Processing

Spatiotemporal feature extraction from phonon microscopy and predictive modelling on MEG signals — interpretable, multi-task neural networks for cell-level insight.

  • Phonon-microscopy cancer-cell analysis
  • Dual-attention spatiotemporal models
  • Interpretable multi-task learning
